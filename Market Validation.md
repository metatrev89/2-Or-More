## Takeaways for 2+ Validation

- **"Daily affirmations" is the head term** — 100K–1M monthly searches confirms a large, active market, but it's High competition and dominated by content/free results.
- **App-intent terms are small but cheap** — "affirmation app" and "manifestation app" (100–1K, Low competition, ~$1–5 bids) are typical of a category where discovery happens in app stores rather than Google Search. App Store Optimization (ASO) likely matters more than SEO for install volume.
- **"Mind movie" is an ownable wedge** — 1K–10K searches with Low competition and cheap bids ($0.34–$3.99). No major player owns this term, and it maps directly to a signature 2+ feature (and the Dispenza audience actively searches it).
- **Demographic long-tail is real** — "…for women" / "…for men" variants each pull 1K–10K, useful for content marketing and onboarding personalization angles.
- **"Manifestation coach" (100–1K, Low, $2.32–$7.60)** — modest search volume for the v3 marketplace, but the higher bid floor suggests commercial intent (coaches pay to be found).
