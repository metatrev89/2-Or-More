# Claude Design Prompt — 2+ v1 Prototype

Build the first design pass of **2+**, a mobile app for AI-driven affirmations and manifestation. Two files are attached — treat them as the source of truth:

1. **2+ Product Overview.pdf** — product concept, user stories (US-1 through US-25), roadmap, brand palette with usage rules, design principles, and reference mockups of three screens.
2. **First-Run Experience.md** — the complete written walkthrough of the first-run flow, screen by screen, with copy examples and the emotional arc.

## What to build

The complete v1 first-run flow plus core app, as a high-fidelity mobile prototype, in this order:

1. Cold open / manifesto (3 screens)
2. Sign up (Apple / Google / email)
3. Conversational AI intake (chat interview across 7 life areas — match the reference mockup)
4. The "build moment" transition (ink screen, generation theater)
5. Affirmation review — the "want → I am" reveal, one affirmation at a time (match the reference mockup)
6. Scheduling setup (recommended "Prime protocol" plan + custom option)
7. Paywall (ink background, user's own affirmations faintly visible, gold CTA)
8. Post-paywall creation moment (voice choice + photo opt-in)
9. Home / daily ritual screen (match the reference mockup closely — it is the approved design)
10. Player (audio + mind movie theater view)
11. Progress / rings dashboard
12. Profile / personal affirmation feed

## Non-negotiable design rules

- **Palette (Golden hour 2.0):** cream canvas #FAF4E8, ink #26201A, gold #E9B84C, teal #157A6E, AI tint #DFF0ED, warm gray #8A7A66, borders #E8DEC9, white cards. Full usage rules in the PDF.
- **Teal is AI-only.** It marks things the AI did or tracks (play button, waveform, chips, progress fills, its chat avatar). Never decorative.
- **Gold is achievement-only** (streaks, rings, medals, weekly bars, mind-movie accents).
- **No red anywhere.** Feedback is always encouraging, never condemning. Missed days pause streaks warmly; no alarm states.
- **Typography:** serif italic for affirmation text (the sacred register), clean sans for UI, monospace for numbers and timestamps.
- **Show the work:** label AI output ("Rewritten as I am" chips, "New scene generated from goal 3"). The app should feel like an intelligent instrument, not a journal.
- **Voice-first:** the mic is the primary input in the intake; keyboard is secondary.
- **Bottom nav, v1:** three tabs only — Home, Progress, Profile. No ghost tabs for future social/coach features.

## Tone of all copy

Warm, direct, spiritually grounded but never preachy. Present tense wherever possible. The AI speaks like a wise, encouraging coach who remembers what you said and references it back. Sample voice is throughout the First-Run Experience doc — match it.

## Where you have latitude

Motion and transitions, empty/loading states, exact paywall pricing layout (prices are placeholder), icon style (outline, consistent), and the manifesto art direction. For the open questions listed at the end of the First-Run Experience doc, make a sensible default and note the decision rather than blocking.

Start with the onboarding flow (screens 1–8), then the home screen, then the rest.
